Design principles

The rules every screen follows.

Motion with physics

Springs instead of fixed timings. Every animation can be interrupted and reversed mid-flight.

Monochrome, two themes

Black, white and greys. Light and dark mode are designed separately, not inverted.

One clear next step

The positive action is always a white button. Skipping is always plain underlined text.
