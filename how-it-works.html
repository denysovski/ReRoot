How it works

Five steps. One of them daily.

Re:Root keeps the routine small so you can keep it up for months. Here's the whole flow, from sign-up to the hard days.

First

Create an account

Enter your name, email and a password. In this demo the account is simulated and stays in your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere.

Every day

Check in

Tap "Stayed clean" or "Slipped", rate the urge from 0 to 5 and write what helped. Missed a day? Tap it in the week row to fix it.

Over weeks

See the pattern

Your streak line, urge strength and a 12-week calendar show when the habit pulls hardest, so you can plan for those moments.

On hard days

Get through the moment

Open the Read tab for a breathing exercise and short guides, or post in a community room. If you slip, log it and start again tomorrow.
