1. What Re:Root is

Re:Root is a free demo web app for tracking habits you want to quit. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no paid plan and no real account system.

2. Simulated accounts

When you "register", your name, email and a hashed version of your password are stored in your browser's local storage. Anyone with access to your device and browser could access them. Do not use a password you use anywhere else.

3. Your content

Check-ins, notes and community posts you create stay on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently. You are responsible for what you write.
