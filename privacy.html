Privacy policy

Short version: your data never leaves your browser.

What we store

  • Account: name, email, a SHA-256 hash of your password, timezone and preferences.
  • Tracking data: habits, daily check-ins, urge ratings and notes.
  • Community posts and contact form messages you write.
  • Settings: theme choice.

Where it's stored

Only in your browser's local storage on this device. Re:Root has no server or database, and no data is sent to us or anyone else.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may log standard request data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. Re:Root uses no analytics, ads or tracking cookies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your browser shows them locally. No push service or server is involved.

Deleting your data

Log out and clear this site's data in your browser settings, or clear all browser data. Deletion is permanent and immediate.
