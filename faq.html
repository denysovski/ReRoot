FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find yours? Send us a message.

Is Re:Root free?

Yes. This is a demo project and every feature is free.

Is my account real?

No. Accounts are simulated. Your name, email and a hashed password are stored in your browser's local storage, so they only exist on this device and browser. Don't reuse a real password.

Can someone else see my check-ins?

No. Nothing is sent to a server. If you share the device and browser, anyone using it could open your account, so log out when you're done.

How do notifications work?

During onboarding, tap "Yes, I want notifications" and your browser asks for permission. If you allow it, Re:Root sends a test notification. You can change this later from the menu in the top right of the app.

What counts as a streak?

Your current streak is the number of clean days in a row up to today. Today doesn't break the streak until you log it. A slip, or a past day you never logged, resets it.

Can I track more than one habit?

Yes, as many as you like. Each habit has its own streak, goal and charts.

I slipped. Should I delete the day?

Please don't. Log it honestly. The pattern of slips is the most useful data you have. Read "A slip resets the counter, not you" in the articles.

Is this medical advice?

No. Re:Root is a self-tracking tool. If a habit is harming your health or you can't stop, talk to a doctor or therapist.
