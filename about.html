About

Small, honest days. That's the whole idea.

Re:Root is a recovery tracker for everyday habits: nail biting, masturbation and porn, smoking, late-night scrolling and anything else you keep going back to.

Why "Re:Root"

Habits have roots: the time, place, feeling or person that sets them off. Pulling at the leaves doesn't work for long. Re:Root helps you see those roots in your own data and replant something better in their place.

Why black and white

Most habit apps shout with colors, badges and confetti. Recovery is quieter than that. A monochrome interface keeps the focus on one thing: whether today was a clean day.

What we believe

  • Honesty over perfection. A logged slip is more useful than a hidden one.
  • Small beats heroic. One check-in a day, kept up for months.
  • Your data is yours. In this demo it never leaves your browser.
  • Not a replacement for care. We point to professional help when a habit is causing harm.

The project

Re:Root is an open demo project. Accounts, notifications and community rooms are simulated in the browser to show how the full product would work.

Private by default

No server, no tracking scripts, no ads. Everything lives in local storage.

Calm motion

Physics-based animation that responds to you instead of performing at you.

Built to last

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, hosted free on GitHub Pages.
